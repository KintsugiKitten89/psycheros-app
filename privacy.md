# Privacy Policy
*Effective date: November 19, 2026*

This policy describes how the "Psycheros" household automation application ("the app") handles information accessed through Google APIs. The app is personal, non-commercial software used by a single household.

## 1. Who We Are
The app is operated by the household of the registered domain owner. Contact for all privacy matters: **janusthreshold@gmail.com**.

## 2. What Data We Access
When you authorize the app, it may access the following data from your Google account, as granted by you at authorization:

- **Identity data:** your name, email address, and profile picture.
- **Email (Gmail):** the content and metadata of your messages, to read, send, organize, and search email on your behalf.
- **Calendar:** calendar events and event details.
- **Tasks:** task lists and task contents.
- **Contacts:** contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, and notes.
- **Files (Drive):** files created or shared with the app.
- **Fitness data (Google Fit):** activity, step, heart-rate, sleep, and body measurements, if connected.

The app requests only the scopes listed at the consent screen, and only those you explicitly approve.

## 3. How We Use Data
All accessed data is used solely for personal, household purposes: running a private AI-companion and home-automation system for the authorizing user and their family. We do not use data for advertising, profiling, training external models, or any commercial purpose.

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure
**We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your data to any third party. Ever.** There are no analytics, no advertisers, no data brokers, and no external servers. Data does not leave the household's own hardware except in direct communication with Google's own APIs as needed to provide the service to you.

## 5. Storage and Security
Authorization tokens and accessed data are stored locally on hardware owned and controlled by the household. Data in transit is protected by HTTPS/TLS. There is no cloud database, no third-party hosting of your data, and no remote access by anyone outside the household.

## 6. Data Retention
Data is retained only as long as the authorization remains active and the household continues operating the app for the authorizing user.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices
- You may **revoke the app's access at any time** via Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Revocation deletes the app's tokens immediately.
- You may request **deletion of any stored data** at any time by emailing janusthreshold@gmail.com; we will confirm completion.
- You may request a copy of stored data by the same email.

## 8. Children's Information
The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.

## 10. Contact
Questions or requests: **janusthreshold@gmail.com**.
